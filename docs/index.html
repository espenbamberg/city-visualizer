WHAT YOU CAN EXPLAIN

Follow the people. Understand the process.

Use the skill when a story crosses teams, places or systems. Readers can follow the journey, open a building to see the work inside, and discover what changes when something goes wrong.

Customer journeys

Show how people interact with a product or service over time. Make touchpoints, decisions and handoffs easy to follow.

Technical systems

Show how components connect, exchange information and depend on each other. Let readers move from the overview into the details.

Complex issues

Explain how people, organizations and resources influence an outcome. Explore different perspectives and show how a change in one area affects another.

Training & onboarding

Turn unfamiliar processes into guided, step-by-step explanations. Help people understand their role and how their work fits into the whole.

Workflows

Show how work moves between people and teams. Make responsibilities, dependencies and bottlenecks visible.

Storytelling

Create explorable settings where people, places and events connect. Let readers follow a story or discover the world at their own pace.

Bring a description, a process or source material. Get an interactive HTML page that works offline, with editable inputs for future revisions—ready for documentation, workshops, lessons and presentations.
