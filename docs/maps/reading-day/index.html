THE ISSUE, IN WORDS

How does a reader move from finding a book to joining a reading group?

Follow a reader through five places, from home to a shared reading session.

SCENARIO 01

Find a book, join a group

Starting condition. The collection and reading room are open.

  1. Walk to welcome desk

    Staff help the reader choose a collection and explain today’s activities.

  2. Walk to book collection

    The reader borrows a book selected with the collection team.

  3. Walk to reading garden

    The reader takes a break in the garden before the group begins.

  4. Walk to reading room

    Readers meet around tables for a shared reading session.

Outcome. The reader arrives with a borrowed book and joins the group.

All places, services and relationships

01 · Reader home

Reader. The reader leaves home for a day of borrowing and a reading group.

The group meets in the afternoon.

02 · Welcome desk

Library staff. Staff help the reader choose a collection and explain today’s activities.

The collection is in a separate building.

03 · Book collection

Collection team. The reader borrows a book selected with the collection team.

The book must be available on the shelf.

04 · Reading garden

Readers. The reader takes a break in the garden before the group begins.

The path connects the library buildings.

05 · Reading room

Group facilitator. Readers meet around tables for a shared reading session.

The facilitator opens the session at the announced time.

  • Reader home → Welcome desk: Walk to welcome desk (movement).
  • Welcome desk → Book collection: Walk to book collection (movement).
  • Book collection → Reading garden: Walk to reading garden (movement).
  • Reading garden → Reading room: Walk to reading room (movement).
