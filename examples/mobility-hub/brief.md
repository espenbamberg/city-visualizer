# A connected neighbourhood

## Question and audience
How can small public spaces share a place without blocking one another? This editable fictional design study is for map authors extending CityVisualizer.

## Spatial frame
A schematic outdoor campus-scale view keeps the shop, shelter, cycle parking, charging bay and garden in one walkable place. The shop is the visual anchor. The small equipment retains normal-sized details. The shelter faces the shared approach; the racks use a different orientation. A fence follows two sides of the garden.

## Stories
Walk from the shop into the shelter, collect a bicycle, pass through the garden gate, or stand beneath the tree canopy. A separate electrical-supply story follows the cable to the charging cabinet. The vehicle approach is a physical road, not a separate road for each relationship.

## Geometry and editing
The examples demonstrate separate visual bounds, obstacle volumes and reserved approaches. New recipes work as entities and scenery. The decorative racks are placed relative to the shelter; trees are repeated at fixed spacing. Edit layout.json and recompose scene.json, then build the ordinary offline page. Moving or rotating an object updates its named ports and compiled story paths during composition.
