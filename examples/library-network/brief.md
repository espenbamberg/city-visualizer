# Regional library service strategy

This example operating model covers six neighbourhoods: Northbank, Riverside, Market quarter, Hill district, Harbour and West gardens. Each has a welcome desk, lending collection, learning centre, stock store and delivery depot: thirty physical workplaces.

The proposed service shares five applications: catalogue, reservations, membership, delivery planning and reader notices. A runtime, records database and message gateway form the technical foundation. These eight objects have distinct dependencies; they are drawn separately from physical workplaces.

Local teams own welcome, lending and learning. Stock and delivery teams coordinate transfers. A pickup notice follows receiving-branch confirmation. Three stories explain a cross-neighbourhood reader journey, a regional reservation and the common technical dependencies. The supplied detail supports seven districts in the illustration: six neighbourhoods plus shared services.
